Geospatial Services

Mapping, analysis, and learning tools grounded in place.

I provide geospatial mapping and analysis services that help organizations, educators, and researchers better understand place. Using open-source tools and place-based frameworks, my work supports spatial analysis, interactive web maps, and decision-ready visualizations that connect land, ecology, history, and community. Projects emphasize accessibility, transparency, and real-world context, supporting thoughtful planning, communication, and action.
